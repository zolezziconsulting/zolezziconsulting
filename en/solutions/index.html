Describe my case

01 — Where we start

With the problem, not the product.

People who arrive asking for a specific category often need a different one. We start by understanding what is failing and by when you need a decision.

Customer service

Response times, channel saturation, and processes that depend on people for repetitive work.

Process automation

Operations run by hand, documented in spreadsheets, that do not scale with volume.

Sales productivity

Sales teams without useful information at the moment of decision, and follow-up lost between tools.

Information management

Scattered data, ungoverned and hard to turn into a defensible decision.

Financial services

Origination, assessment and compliance processes with regulatory demands the current tooling does not cover.

Applied artificial intelligence

Where it genuinely adds value in your operation, and where it is just an expensive project with no return.

Risk and security

Fraud, operational continuity, access control and exposure of critical information.

02 — How it works

From the problem to the decision.

We do not sell licences and we do not charge for the search. We are compensated by the technology vendor, so exploring an option costs you nothing.

  1. 01

    You describe the challenge

    The specific problem, what you have already tried and what constraints you have.

  2. 02

    We find the technology

    Among the solutions we know and, where needed, in the international market.

  3. 03

    Demo and pilot

    We coordinate the demonstration with the vendor and, where it makes sense, a proof of concept in your own environment.

  4. 04

    Decision and rollout

    You contract directly with the vendor. We stay in the conversation.
