01 — The model

Entering a market is not having a translated deck.

A solution that already works elsewhere rarely fails in Peru for technical reasons. It fails because nobody local knows who to call, what budget exists, or at what point in the year the decision is actually made.

  1. 01

    We learn the solution

    What it does, who it serves, how it sells today and what makes it different from what is already here.

  2. 02

    We assess the fit

    We test the proposition against what the Peruvian market buys, its regulatory context and its level of maturity. This can end in a no.

  3. 03

    We map the opportunity

    Segments, types of organisation and the functions with a problem your solution genuinely solves.

  4. 04

    We define the partnership

    Scope, working model, responsibilities and next steps. In writing, before anything starts.

  5. 05

    We develop the business

    First conversations, commercial follow-up and relationship building with the accounts that move forward.

03 — Why a local partner

Someone already in the room.

We know the buyer

How a Peruvian organisation decides, who signs, which functions have to weigh in, and how long that path really takes.

Presence without fixed structure

Commercial activity in the country without incorporating an entity or hiring a team while the opportunity is still being validated.

A single counterpart

The vendor talks to one contact and so does the Peruvian customer. Nobody is left between two parties who do not understand each other.

We assess before we promise

If we do not see a path for your solution in this market, we tell you in the first conversation.

04 — What we look for

The kind of solution we work with.

We work with a small number of solutions at a time so each one gets real attention. We do not publish brand names: until an agreement allows it, naming a vendor on this site would claim a relationship that is not formalised.

A product in real use

With customers in production in at least one market. We do not bring the Peruvian buyer technology that is still being validated.

Long-cycle B2B sales

Solutions bought by committees and technical functions, not individual users. That is where a local partner changes the outcome.

A recognisable problem

One the Peruvian customer can name without being taught the category first. If the category has to be created, the path is a different one.

Willingness to show up

Demos and proofs of concept with your technical team. We provide the commercial presence; the product depth stays yours.
